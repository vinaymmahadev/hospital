Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP)

Discovering that one is pregnant can be a cherished and emotional moment for parents-to-be. However, in certain situations, continuing the pregnancy may not be safe due to risks to the mother’s health, potential complications, or other medical reasons. In such cases, pregnancy termination may be advised. This can be carried out either through prescribed medications or a minor surgical procedure. In the early stages of pregnancy, termination using medicines is generally considered a safer option, while pregnancies beyond 9 weeks may require a surgical procedure such as Dilation and Curettage (D & C) to ensure the mother’s safety.

What is Medical Termination of Pregnancy?

Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP) is a safe and medically approved method of ending a pregnancy under the supervision of a qualified gynecologist. It involves the use of specific medicines that help stop the pregnancy from continuing and allow the uterus to expel the pregnancy tissue naturally. MTP is commonly recommended during the early stages of pregnancy, usually within the first trimester. The procedure is planned carefully based on the woman’s health condition and pregnancy duration to ensure maximum safety and minimal complications.

Reasons for Medical Termination

  • Risk to the mother’s health or life
  • High-risk pregnancy or complications
  • Fetal or genetic abnormalities
  • Incomplete miscarriage
  • Unplanned or unwanted pregnancy

Every woman’s circumstances are unique, and the reasons for considering a pregnancy termination can vary greatly. Choosing to undergo an abortion is a significant decision that may have both physical and emotional impacts. It is important to consult a qualified doctor who can guide you through the available options, explain the risks and benefits, and ensure your safety. Seeking support from trusted family members or close friends can also help you make an informed and confident decision.

How Is Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP) Performed?

Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP) is a safe and legally approved procedure carried out under medical guidance. The method chosen depends on the stage of pregnancy and is always decided after a detailed consultation with the doctor.

Medical Abortion

Medical abortion is usually recommended during the early weeks of pregnancy, typically up to 9 weeks. This method involves the use of prescribed oral medicines that help terminate the pregnancy naturally.

An MTP kit is provided by the doctor along with clear instructions. After taking the medication, bleeding and mild cramps are a normal part of the process. A follow-up visit is important to ensure the procedure is complete and recovery is on track.

Surgical Abortion

When the pregnancy has progressed beyond 9 weeks, a surgical method may be advised. These procedures are performed by experienced specialists in a controlled and safe medical setting.

Suction Procedure: This method can be performed up to 15 weeks of pregnancy and usually takes about 5 to 10 minutes.

Dilatation and Evacuation (D&E): Recommended after 13 weeks of pregnancy, this procedure generally takes around 15 to 30 minutes and is carried out with utmost care.

What Happens During a Medical Abortion?

A medical abortion is a non-surgical method used to end an early pregnancy with the help of prescribed medicines. These medicines may be taken orally or vaginally, strictly under a doctor’s guidance, to ensure safety and effectiveness.

The process involves two medicines. The first medicine, Mifepristone, works by blocking the hormone progesterone, which is essential for continuing a pregnancy. When this hormone is blocked, the lining of the uterus becomes thin, preventing the pregnancy from growing further.

The second medicine, Misoprostol, is usually taken 24 to 48 hours after Mifepristone. This medicine causes the uterus to contract and helps in expelling the pregnancy tissue.

Some women may experience abdominal cramps, bleeding with blood clots, mild fever or chills, tiredness, nausea, diarrhea, dizziness, or vomiting. These effects are expected and vary from person to person.

What Is an MTP Kit?

An MTP kit is used for medical termination of pregnancy during the early stages, usually between 7 and 9 weeks of gestation. The kit contains Mifepristone and Misoprostol, which work together to safely end the pregnancy.

Some women may experience nausea, abdominal pain, vomiting, or vaginal bleeding. A follow-up consultation is important to ensure the abortion is complete and recovery is normal.

Who Can Conduct an Abortion?

As per Indian law, a medical termination of pregnancy can only be performed by qualified medical professionals, including registered medical practitioners, trained obstetricians, and gynecologists with required experience.

Who Is Eligible to Undergo MTP?

Women who wish to terminate an unwanted pregnancy and are in the early stages, usually up to nine weeks, are eligible for medical abortion. In special circumstances defined by Indian law, abortion may be permitted up to 24 weeks after medical evaluation.

Who Should Not or Cannot Undergo MTP?

Medical abortion may not be suitable for everyone. It is not recommended in cases of advanced pregnancy beyond 20–24 weeks, ectopic pregnancy, allergy to abortion medicines, or certain medical conditions.

Women with blood clotting disorders, severe anaemia, seizure disorders, chronic illnesses, long-term steroid use, IUD in place, or serious heart, kidney, or blood pressure conditions should consult a specialist before opting for MTP.

What Are the Benefits of Medical Abortion?

Medical abortion is safe and effective up to 9 weeks of pregnancy. It involves relatively mild pain, avoids surgery, feels more natural, and can often be completed at home with emotional support from loved ones.

What Are the Risks of Medical Abortion?

When taken correctly, medical abortion is safe. However, improper use may cause incomplete abortion, heavy bleeding, digestive discomfort, diarrhea, allergic reactions, or vaginal infections.

Persistent fever, foul vaginal odor, or bleeding lasting more than 48 hours should be reported to a doctor immediately.

Recovery After MTP

Mifepristone and Misoprostol are effective in up to 98% of cases. Most women start feeling better once bleeding reduces. Normal activities can be resumed after bleeding stops completely.

Adequate rest is advised. Consult your gynecologist if you experience severe bleeding, fever, vomiting, or unusual symptoms.

What to Expect After a Medical Abortion?

Bleeding and cramping are common and may last for several days, similar to menstruation. In most cases, bleeding reduces by the second or third week.

A follow-up visit or ultrasound scan may be scheduled within 2–5 weeks to confirm that the abortion is complete.

Is Medical Abortion Legal in India?

Yes, medical abortion is legal in India under the Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP) Act, 1971. Approved medicines may be used up to 9 weeks of pregnancy.

The MTP Amendment Act, 2021 allows abortion up to 24 weeks in specific circumstances, based on medical opinion and with the woman’s consent.

What is Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP)?

Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP) is a medically approved procedure to end an early pregnancy using prescribed medicines. Does MTP require surgery?

No, medical termination of pregnancy does not involve surgery. It is done using approved abortion medicines as prescribed by a gynecologist. The process mimics a natural miscarriage. Hospital admission is usually not required. This makes MTP a preferred choice for early pregnancy termination.
