What is Medical Termination of Pregnancy?

Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP), also known as medical abortion, is a safe and non-surgical method used to end an early pregnancy with the help of prescribed medicines. Instead of a surgical procedure, this method uses two approved medications that work together to stop the pregnancy from continuing.

Pregnancy depends on the hormone progesterone to grow and develop. The medicines used in medical abortion block the action of this hormone, preventing further development of the pregnancy. Medical abortion can be done soon after pregnancy is confirmed and is usually recommended up to the 8th or 9th week.

MTP is considered a low-risk and minimally invasive option with a success rate of nearly 99% in early pregnancies. According to data reported by ScienceDirect, over 3.9 lakh medical terminations were recorded in India between April 2018 and March 2019, highlighting its widespread and safe usage among women of reproductive age.

What Happens During Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP)?

Medical termination of pregnancy is a step-by-step, medically supervised process carried out under the guidance of a qualified gynecologist. The procedure begins with a detailed consultation, during which the doctor evaluates your overall health and confirms the stage of pregnancy.

As part of the assessment, blood tests and ultrasound scans may be advised to ensure that medical abortion is a safe and suitable option for you. Since abortion is a deeply personal decision, the gynecologist explains the procedure in detail, including possible physical and emotional changes, so you can make an informed choice.

Before proceeding, your doctor will discuss pain expectations, aftercare, and recovery. You will then be asked to sign a consent form that clearly outlines the process and post-abortion guidelines.

The medical abortion involves two approved medicines. The first tablet, Mifepristone, works by blocking the hormone progesterone, which is necessary for pregnancy to continue.

After 24 to 48 hours, the second medicine, Misoprostol, is taken. This medicine helps soften the cervix and allows the uterus to expel the pregnancy tissue.

Bleeding and cramping are common and may feel similar to a heavy menstrual period. If there is no bleeding within 24 hours after taking Misoprostol, contact your doctor immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP)?

Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP) is a medically approved procedure to end an early pregnancy using prescribed medicines. It is a non-surgical method performed under the supervision of a certified gynecologist. The procedure works by blocking pregnancy-supporting hormones and inducing uterine contractions. MTP is commonly chosen for its safety, privacy, and effectiveness. At Rite Surgery, MTP is provided following all legal and medical guidelines.

Is MTP safe?

Yes, MTP is a safe and low-risk procedure when done under medical supervision. It has a very high success rate when performed within the recommended gestational period. Complications are rare if guidelines are followed correctly. Proper consultation and follow-up ensure patient safety. Our gynecologists prioritize comfort, confidentiality, and well-being.

Up to how many weeks can MTP be done?

Medical abortion is generally recommended up to 8–9 weeks of pregnancy. The exact eligibility depends on medical evaluation and ultrasound findings. Early consultation helps ensure safer outcomes. Beyond this period, alternative methods may be advised. Our doctors guide you with the safest available options.

Does MTP require surgery?

No, medical termination of pregnancy does not involve surgery. It is done using approved abortion medicines as prescribed by a gynecologist. The process mimics a natural miscarriage. Hospital admission is usually not required. This makes MTP a preferred choice for early pregnancy termination.

Will there be pain or bleeding?

Mild to moderate cramping and bleeding are normal during MTP. The experience is similar to heavy menstrual bleeding. Pain varies from person to person and can be managed with medication. Bleeding may last a few days to one or two weeks. Doctors provide guidance on what is normal and when to seek help.

How long does the MTP process take?

The consultation and medicine intake usually take about 20–30 minutes. The abortion process completes within a few hours to a couple of days after medication. Recovery is gradual and differs for each individual. Most women can resume normal activities within a day or two. Follow-up is advised to confirm completion.

Is privacy maintained?

Yes, complete privacy and confidentiality are strictly maintained at Rite Surgery. Patient information is protected at every step of the process. Consultations and treatments are handled discreetly. Only authorized medical professionals are involved. Respect for patient dignity is our top priority.

Is MTP legal in India?

Yes, MTP is legal in India under the Medical Termination of Pregnancy Act. It must be performed by a registered medical practitioner at a licensed facility. Legal limits depend on gestational age and medical conditions. All procedures at Rite Surgery comply with current legal amendments. Proper documentation and consent are followed.

Is MTP covered by insurance?

Coverage for MTP may vary depending on the insurance provider and policy terms. Some plans cover abortion when medically indicated. Our support team can help clarify insurance eligibility. Transparent cost details are shared upfront. Affordable care options are also available.

What care is needed after MTP?

Rest and proper hydration are recommended after the procedure. Avoid heavy physical activity for a few days. Follow prescribed medications and hygiene instructions carefully. A follow-up visit helps confirm complete recovery. Emotional support is equally important and available if needed.
